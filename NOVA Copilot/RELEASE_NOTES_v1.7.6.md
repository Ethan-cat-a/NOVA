# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.6

- The edge grip is attached to the open assistant drawer and follows it while moving.
- Pull left from the edge tab to open. Push the attached grip right to tuck the drawer away.
- The grab tab returns to the screen edge when the drawer is closed.

Build 36 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
