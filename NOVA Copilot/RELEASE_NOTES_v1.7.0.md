# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.0

- Gives NOVA’s glowing blue orb a friendly pair of eyes in the menu bar, app header, and assistant replies.
- Reworks the hidden edge launcher into a compact avatar peek that expands on hover.
- Adds a draggable grip for repositioning the edge launcher vertically and remembers its position.
- Smooths the assistant panel’s slide, fade, and settle transitions.
- Keeps the Sparkle sandbox installer entitlement fix from 1.6.9.

Build 30 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
