# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.1

- Keeps the collapsed side launcher visible by showing the avatar at the screen edge.
- Widens the edge peek so the avatar is easy to spot and open.
- Expands the full launcher onto the screen when hovered, keeping its drag grip usable.

Build 31 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
