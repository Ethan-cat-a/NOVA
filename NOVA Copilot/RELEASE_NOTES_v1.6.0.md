# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.6.0

## My Documents, when you need it

- Add one or more folders containing PDFs, Word, RTF, Markdown or text files.
- Turn on **My Documents** from the books button for the next message only.
- NOVA searches those folders on your Mac and sends up to four relevant excerpts with that request.
- Turn it off or leave it off and document text is not sent. Document contents are also kept out of saved chat history.
- Replies show a collapsed source-details section for the documents used.
