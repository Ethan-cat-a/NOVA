# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.7

This release simplifies the side drawer and fixes the disappearing or detached handle.

- Uses one window for the assistant panel and its edge grip, so they stay attached.
- Keeps a 64-pixel grip visible at the right edge while the drawer is tucked away.
- Drag left to reveal NOVA; drag right to tuck it back. Click the grip to toggle it.
- Keeps the menu-bar icon and ⌘⇧Space shortcut as alternate ways to open or tuck the drawer.

The universal macOS build supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later.
