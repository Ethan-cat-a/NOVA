# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.2

- Restores the side launcher on the primary display at startup and after the app or display configuration changes.
- Keeps the non-activating floating launcher visible when NOVA loses focus.
- Adds **Window → Show NOVA Side Launcher** as a manual recovery option.
- Keeps the avatar peek visible and brings the full launcher and drag grip onscreen on hover.

Build 32 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
