# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.4

- Keeps the draggable side launcher visible at the screen edge while the assistant panel is open or closed.
- Reserves space beside the chat so the launcher does not cover its controls.
- Restores the launcher after startup, app activation and display changes; **Window → Show NOVA Side Launcher** remains available as a recovery option.
- The edge avatar toggles NOVA, and the launcher keeps the drag and vertical positioning behavior from 1.7.3.
- Retains the owner-account recovery improvements included in the previous development build.

Build 34 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
