# NOVA Copilot for macOS 1.7.3

- Drag the visible edge tab left to pull the full launcher onscreen; drag it right to tuck it back to the edge.
- Drag anywhere on the visible tab or expanded launcher to position it vertically.
- Keeps the launcher expanded after a pull-out until it is dragged back. The expanded state survives app updates.
- If an owner code was already claimed on another device, sign in with the owner Google account to restore unlimited access on this device. The server-side owner account survives app updates and works across devices.

Build 33 · macOS 13 or later · Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may ask you to Control-click the app and choose Open on first launch.
